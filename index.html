About Us

IMEDHUB is the academic portal of our college: notes, attendance, assignments, fees, exam forms, library and more in one login.

The problem

Students often have to ask friends, check notice boards or search many different places just to find a syllabus, an exam date or an old question paper.

Our solution

IMEDHUB keeps everything on one website. The admin uploads the information once and every registered student can see it any time.

What you get after login

These four sections open only for registered students.

📘

Syllabus

Semester-wise course list with course type, credits and marks distribution. You can also search for a course.
📅

Timetable

The yearly academic calendar: semester start dates, exams, practicals, holidays and events.
📄

PYQ

Previous year question papers added by the admin, so you can practise before your exams.
⬇️

Downloads

Notes, assignments and other important documents in one place.

How to get started

Register

Click Sign Up, enter your name, email and a password of at least eight characters.

Login

Use the same email and password. The menu and your personal dashboard will open.

Study

Open the syllabus, check exam dates, download papers and read the latest announcements.

Who can use it?

👩‍🎓 Students

  • Register and login with email
  • View syllabus and academic calendar
  • Open PYQ, senior notes and assignments
  • Track attendance, results, library books and fees
  • Fill the exam form online and download receipts
  • Read the latest announcements

🛠️ Admin

  • Add syllabus, timetable, PYQ and download links
  • Post announcements for students
  • Delete old or wrong entries

Common Questions

Is IMEDHUB free to use?

Yes. You only need to create an account with your email.

Why can't I open the Syllabus page?

The pages are available only after you register and login. If you are not logged in you will be sent to the login page.

I forgot to logout. Is that a problem?

Click the Logout button in the top right corner when you finish, especially on a shared computer.

Who adds the PYQ and downloads?

Only the admin can add them. If something is missing, please ask your admin to upload it.
